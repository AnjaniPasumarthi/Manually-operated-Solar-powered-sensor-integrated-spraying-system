# Manually-operated-Solar-powered-sensor-integrated-spraying-system
B.tech project on manually operated solar powered sensor integrated spraying system for efficient and uniform agricultural spraying.

## About the Project

The main purpose of the system is to provide controlled and efficient spraying while reducing unnecessary pesticide usage and improving spraying performance.

## Objectives

- To develop a manually operated agricultural spraying system.
- To integrate sensor technology into the spraying system.
- To utilize solar energy as a power source.
- To develop a simple and economical system suitable for agricultural applications.

## Key Features

- Solar-powered operation
- Sensor-integrated spraying mechanism
- Manually operated system
- Improved spraying uniformity
- Efficient pesticide application
- Reduced energy consumption
- Suitable for agricultural field applications

## Components Used

The major components used in the system include:

- Solar panel

- Spray tank
- Pump

- Control and electrical components

## Working Principle

The solar panel converts solar energy into electrical energy, which is used to charge the battery. The stored electrical energy powers the spraying system.

During operation, the manually operated system is moved through the field. The integrated sensor helps in controlling the spraying operation according to the required field conditions. The pump delivers the spraying liquid from the tank through the nozzles, producing the required spray.

The system is designed to improve spraying efficiency and achieve more uniform application compared with conventional manual spraying methods.

## Methodology

The development of the system involved the following major steps:

1. Identification of the problems associated with conventional spraying.
2. Selection of suitable components.
3. Design and fabrication of the spraying system.
4. Integration of the solar power system.
5. Integration of the sensor into the spraying mechanism.
6. Assembly of the pump, tank, pipes and nozzles.
7. Testing of the developed system.
8. Evaluation of spraying performance and uniformity.

## Experimental Results

The developed spraying system was tested to evaluate its spraying performance.

The experiments focused on parameters such as:

- Sensor performance
- Spray uniformity
- Pesticide discharge rate
- Spraying performance with and without sensor integration

The test results demonstrated the potential of the developed system for efficient and uniform agricultural spraying.
